Our Story

Waterfront Soaps began with sunrise walks along the marina and the desire to capture that gentle, salt-air calm in a bar of soap. We are a family-run studio preparing a peaceful storefront where every shelf feels like a breath of ocean air.

Our team hand-blends each recipe with skin-loving oils, mineral-rich clays, and a touch of coastal botanicals. The result is a collection of soaps and body rituals that feel restorative, grounding, and quietly luxurious.

Naturally derived ingredients Cruelty-free, mindful sourcing Hand-poured in small batches

Behind the Scenes

While our shop is getting its finishing touches, we are busy testing new formulas, designing sustainable packaging, and building a space where customers can gather, sample, and unwind.

Slow Curing Process

Our soaps rest for weeks so every bar is gentle, long-lasting, and perfectly balanced.

Coastal Botanicals

We lean into sea minerals, lavender, citrus zest, and airy florals to evoke the shoreline.

Thoughtful Packaging

Soft textures, recycled papers, and a hint of shimmer make every gift feel curated and calm.
